# Product Brief
## Vision
Build a trusted, fast B2B pharmaceutical procurement platform so licensed medicine retailers can order from one digital experience with transparent availability and dependable delivery.

## MVP
Retailer app, admin/operations web panel, warehouse workflow, procurement queue, delivery partner interface. Suppliers are not marketplace sellers; all goods must arrive at and be checked by Taneja's warehouse before dispatch.

## Goals
1. Reduce phone-based ordering friction.
2. Improve stock visibility and order accuracy.
3. Increase repeat ordering.
4. Track fulfillment and delivery exceptions.
5. Support special-order requests.
6. Validate metrics before geographic expansion.

## Product principles
Simple, bilingual, mobile-first, configurable, auditable. Do not claim “every medicine” or “fastest in India” until operational evidence supports it.
